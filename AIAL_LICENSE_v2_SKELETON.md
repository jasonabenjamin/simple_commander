# AIAL — AI-Attribution License v2 (Working Draft)

**Status:** Working skeleton only (not final legal text)

This file is included with Simple Commander to document the project's use of the AI-Attribution License (AIAL) v2 framework. The upstream AIAL v2 project describes this text as an early-stage conceptual exploration rather than finalized legal prose.

## 0. Preamble (non-operative)

This License is intended to provide a permissive grant for software distribution while allowing Contributors to make provenance declarations about Covered Contributions and, where expressly stated, to adopt a limited no-claim posture for certain declared generated-origin contributions.

## 1. Definitions

### 1.1 “Contributor”
A person or legal entity that distributes, publishes, or makes available a Covered Contribution under this License.

### 1.2 “Covered Contribution”
A file, generated artifact, release element, or other identified contribution distributed under this License by a Contributor.

### 1.3 “Recipient”
Any person or entity receiving a Covered Contribution under this License.

### 1.4 “Provenance Declaration”
A declaration made by a Contributor or Originator regarding the stated provenance of a Covered Contribution.

### 1.5 “Originator”
The person or entity making the Provenance Declaration for a Covered Contribution.

### 1.6 “Generated-Origin Declaration”
A Provenance Declaration stating that the Contributor or Originator designates the Covered Contribution as `generated-origin` under the accompanying provenance specification or equivalent declaration.

## 2. Permission Grant

Subject to the terms of this License, and to the extent the Contributor holds rights in the Covered Contribution, the Contributor hereby grants to each Recipient a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use, copy, modify, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense, and/or sell copies of the Covered Contribution, and to permit persons to whom the Covered Contribution is furnished to do so.

## 3. Notice Preservation

Recipients must preserve this License text and any applicable copyright or attribution notices in all copies or substantial portions of the Covered Contribution, except where such preservation is not reasonably possible for generated artifacts or machine-transformed outputs, in which case equivalent notice in accompanying documentation or manifests is sufficient.

## 4. Provenance Declarations

A Contributor may provide Provenance Declarations for Covered Contributions.

Provenance Declarations:

- are informational representations by the declaring party;
- do not by themselves determine the legal status of the Covered Contribution;
- do not negate third-party rights; and
- do not expand permissions beyond rights held by the declaring Contributor.

## 5. Optional No-Claim / Covenant for Declared Generated-Origin Contributions

Where a Contributor expressly designates a Covered Contribution as subject to an additional no-claim declaration, the Contributor states that, to the extent the Contributor holds any copyright or similar exclusive rights in that Covered Contribution, the Contributor:

- does not assert such rights against Recipients exercising permissions granted under this License; and
- covenants not to assert such rights against such exercise.

If any such disclaimer, waiver, or covenant is ineffective under applicable law, the Contributor grants the broadest additional permission permitted by applicable law to achieve substantially the same practical result.

## 6. No Inference from Silence or Ambiguity

The absence, ambiguity, incompleteness, or staleness of any Provenance Declaration does not reduce ordinary legal protections, imply that a Covered Contribution is generated-origin, imply that a no-claim posture applies, or affect third-party or unknown rights.

## 7. Third-Party and Inherited Content

Nothing in this License or in any Provenance Declaration shall be construed to alter upstream license obligations, negate third-party rights, or supersede separate licenses applicable to imported, inherited, or vendored code.

THE COVERED CONTRIBUTION IS PROVIDED “AS IS”, WITHOUT WARRANTY OF ANY KIND, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING BUT NOT LIMITED TO THE WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND NONINFRINGEMENT.

## 10. Companion Provenance Specification

Contributors may use the companion AIAL Provenance Declaration Specification or any substantially equivalent declaration format. Use of a companion specification is recommended for clarity but is not required for the validity of this draft.

## Drafting warnings

- Do not add AI-training conditions here.
- Do not make provenance labels self-executing legal conclusions.
- Do not require future SPDX adoption.
- Keep the permission grant familiar and conservative.
